User guide

  1. Open the app to see a Hangul clock that follows your device's current time and local time zone.
  2. Tap the clock face once to open settings. You can change the theme, date and seconds display, text size, and clock display dimming.
  3. Use your iPhone in portrait or landscape. Your preferences are kept when you reopen the app.
  4. Settings also let you choose flip animation, sound, and whether to keep the screen awake while using the app.

The clock face always shows time and dates in Korean. Settings and guides follow your device's preferred language, with English, Korean, and Japanese supported.

Keep the app open in the foreground to use it as a clock. It does not replace your iPhone's Lock Screen or built-in StandBy screen. The clock and its themes work without an internet connection.

The time looks wrong

Check your iPhone's current time and time zone under Settings → General → Date & Time. The app follows your device's time zone; it is not fixed to Korean time. After changing the time zone, bring the app to the foreground again.

The app or settings stopped responding

Close the app and open it again. If the issue continues, email us your device model, iOS version, and the steps that cause the issue.

Adjusting brightness and sound

The app's dimming setting changes the appearance of the clock display. To change your device's overall screen brightness, use your iPhone's Control Center. You can turn clock sound on or off in the app's settings.

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Applies to: Hangul Moment for iOS 1.0.0(3) and 1.0.1

This notice describes data handling by version. Version 1.0.0(3) has no advertising measurement SDKs. Version 1.0.1 includes code prepared for future integration, but advertising measurement remains disabled.

1. Information used by the clock and preferences

The app reads the system's current time and time zone to display the time and date. It does not use location permission. Clock time and date content and saved preferences are not sent outside your device.

Your theme, date and seconds display, text size, dimming, flip animation, sound, keep-screen-awake preference, and whether the first-use guide has been shown are stored locally on your device in the app's own settings storage.

2. Version 1.0.0(3)

This version has no accounts, app server, advertising or analytics SDKs, or tracking features. The operator does not collect personal information or usage records through this version. It does not request access to photos, contacts, the microphone, location, or notifications.

3. Version 1.0.1 — advertising measurement disabled

Version 1.0.1 includes Meta App Events and TikTok App Events SDKs in preparation for future advertising measurement, but this version does not start the SDKs or request tracking permission. The app does not send personal information or usage activity off your device. All clock features work offline. There are no accounts or in-app ads, and the app does not request access to photos, contacts, the microphone, location, or notifications. Measurement cannot be enabled remotely in this version.

4. Possible future measurement-enabled version — does not apply to 1.0.0 or 1.0.1

If a future version enables advertising measurement, the Meta App Events and TikTok App Events SDKs will be used to measure installations and app launches resulting from our ads. There are no in-app ads or accounts. Clock time and date content and preferences are not sent.

The SDKs start only in a release with measurement enabled and after you grant Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. Installation, app launch and activation activity, advertising identifiers (IDFA) and device identifiers, and device, network and diagnostic information, such as an IP address, may then be sent to Meta and TikTok. These providers use the information for advertising measurement and install attribution.

If you do not initially allow tracking, the advertising measurement SDKs do not start. Withdrawing permission stops new measurement events sent by the app, but the SDKs may continue processing or sending information already received or stored. All clock and theme features remain available offline regardless of your choice. You can change permission in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Each recipient's policies govern the processing, retention, and deletion of information sent to it. See the Meta Privacy Policy and TikTok for Business Privacy Policy, and TikTok Business Products (Data) Terms. Deleting the app or withdrawing permission does not automatically delete information already sent. Send privacy requests, including access or deletion requests, to the support email above or through the relevant provider's privacy contact channels.

5. Saved preferences and deletion

Preferences remain on your device while you use the app. Deleting the app from your device also removes its local preferences. Device backups and restores follow your iOS settings and Apple's policies.

6. Email support

If you choose to contact us by email, your email address and message are sent through the email service. The operator reads them to respond to your inquiry. The app does not automatically send emails or inquiry content. For requests about information in your inquiry, contact the support address above.

8. Changes and contact
